# ⚓ Icebreaker
**Tear through the frozen wastes and keep your trade routes open all winter long.**

In the harsh winters of Vintage Story, your sailboat is often sidelined the moment the water turns to ice. **Icebreaker** changes that, allowing your vessels to punch through ice sheets and navigate frozen lakes, provided you have equipped them for the task.

### 🛠️ How it Works
To break ice, your boat requires more than just momentum. It needs a reinforced prow. 
1. **Craft a Metal Figurehead:** Use metal plates and a standard wooden figurehead to craft a reinforced version.
2. **Equip your Boat:** Attach the Metal Figurehead to your sailboat or raft.
3. **Set Sail:** As you move, the reinforced figurehead will automatically shatter ice blocks in your path, leaving a clear wake behind you.

### ✨ Key Features
*   **Reinforced Prow Mechanic:** Ice breaking is only active when a Metal Figurehead is equipped. This gives you a clear progression for upgrading your vessel.
*   **Standardized Compatibility:** Works with the vanilla Sailboat, Raft, and even boats currently under construction.
*   **Material Tiers:** Support for all major metals: Copper, Tin Bronze, Bismuth Bronze, Black Bronze, Iron, Meteoric Iron, and Steel.
*   **Variety of Styles:** Available for all figurehead types including Bear, Wolf, Skull, and Seamonster.
*   **Performance Focused:** Optimized code ensures the ice breaking logic only runs when necessary, keeping your server running smoothly.

### ⚒️ Crafting
Find the full recipes in the **Handbook (H)** in-game. 
General recipe pattern:
*   `Metal Plates` + `Chisel` + `Hammer` + `Saw` + `Wooden Figurehead`

### 📦 Installation
*   **Mod Type:** Required on both **Server and Client**.
*   **VS 1.22+**: Use the release built on .NET 10 (Folder: 1.22).
*   **VS 1.21.6**: Use the release built on .NET 8 (Folder: 1.21.6).
